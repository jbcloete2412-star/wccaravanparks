Best Western Cape Caravan Parks 2026

Ten parks worth the drive — chosen for scenery, character, and camping experiences that stay with you

Home › Guides › Top 10 Western Cape Parks

The Western Cape has more caravan parks per kilometre of coastline than almost anywhere in Africa. The challenge isn’t finding a park — it’s choosing the right one. We’ve camped at dozens and narrowed it to ten that genuinely stand out.

1. Tietiesbaai Rest Camp, Paternoster

As off-grid as it gets in the Western Cape. The Cape Columbine Nature Reserve has no electricity, no cell signal, and some of the most dramatic Atlantic coast scenery in South Africa. Come during crayfish season (November–February) and you’ll understand why people book a year in advance. Full details →

2. Keurbooms Lagoon Caravan Park, Plettenberg Bay

The biggest and best-positioned park on the Garden Route — 270 sites on the Keurbooms River lagoon, with surf nearby, dolphins year-round, and whale sightings from June. Full details →

3. Kleinfontein, Cederberg

The Cederberg is in a different category entirely. No light pollution, San rock paintings a short hike away, and silence that takes adjustment. The stargazing alone is worth the gravel road. Full details →

4. West Coast National Park, Langebaan

August and September transform the Postberg section into a carpet of wildflowers. Outside flower season, prime birdwatching on the Langebaan lagoon and clean beaches with none of the Cape Town crowds. Full details →

5. Ebb & Flow, Wilderness National Park

SANParks manages this one well. The Touw River runs through the camp, you can canoe from your site, and the forest is dense enough to feel off-grid despite being 10 minutes from Wilderness village. Full details →

6. Jongensfontein Caravan Park, Hessequa

Hessequa Municipality’s flagship park. Still Bay, Blue Flag beaches, and a lagoon mouth ideal for children. Immaculately maintained. Full details →

7. Ellensrust Caravan Park, Still Bay

338 sites along the Goukou River. Blue Flag beach access, tidal pool, surf nearby, and a proper small-town atmosphere. Better value than most Garden Route parks. Full details →

8. Slanghoek Mountain Resort, Rawsonville

Drive 90 minutes from Cape Town and you’re in a valley that looks like it belongs in the Alps. Hot tubs fed by mountain springs, vine-covered hillsides, and wine routes 20 minutes away. Best in autumn. Full details →

9. Imhoff Caravan Park, Kommetjie

The closest serious camping to Cape Town that doesn’t compromise on setting. Chapman’s Peak visible from your stand, Long Beach 150m away, and Kommetjie village for evening walks. Full details →

10. Karoo National Park, Beaufort West

Black rhino, 200+ bird species, a 4x4 trail, and the darkest skies of any national park in the Western Cape. Stop here on your way to the Garden Route — budget at least two nights. Full details →
