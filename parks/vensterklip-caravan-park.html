About Vensterklip Caravan Park

On the edge of a UNESCO World Heritage Site next to the Verlorenvlei wetland, 5km from the famous 6-star Elands Bay left-hand surf break. Rich in San rock art and cave dwellings. One of the wildest and most beautiful coastal settings in the Western Cape — sunsets and crayfish are legendary.

Facilities & What to Expect

Vensterklip Caravan Park in Elands Bay is a basic, municipal facility serving a town that exists primarily for its world-famous surf break. Electric sites are available, ablutions are functional, and the setting on the West Coast is raw and windswept in the most honest sense. This is not a family resort — it is a base camp for surfers, rock fishermen, and those who appreciate the extreme natural beauty of a remote West Coast headland. The town itself is small with minimal services — fuel and basic groceries are the extent of it.

When to Visit

June through August is the season for the legendary Elands Bay left-hand point break — swells arrive from the Southern Ocean and the wind is typically offshore (east) in winter, creating the conditions the surf break is internationally famous for. Spring (September to October) remains a viable surf option and the surrounding West Coast landscape is beautiful in wildflower season. Avoid summer if you are coming specifically for surf — the south-easter blows onshore and degrades conditions.

What to Do Nearby

The Elands Bay left-hand point break is one of the longest natural point breaks in South Africa and appears on surfing bucket lists worldwide — it can run for several hundred metres in good swell. The Baboon's Cave rock art site (2km north) contains San paintings of national significance and is one of the finest accessible rock art sites on the West Coast — accessible via a short walk with a small entry fee. Verlorenvlei (the large freshwater lake adjacent to the town) is an Important Bird Area with flamingos and extraordinary waterfowl. Citrusdal (75km east) and the Cederberg are reachable for day trips.

Getting There

From Cape Town take the N7 north to Piketberg (120km), then the R365 west through Piketberg Mountain to Elands Bay (50km more). Total distance from Cape Town: approximately 170km, around 2 hours. The R365 between Piketberg and Elands Bay is partially gravel — suitable for high-clearance vehicles; standard passenger cars can manage in dry conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Elands Bay suitable for beginner surfers?

Elands Bay's main left-hand point break is a powerful, fast wave best suited to intermediate and advanced surfers, particularly in optimal winter swell. The town beach area can produce gentler beginner conditions. Beginners wanting to learn to surf on the West Coast are better served by Cape Town's more consistent beginner beaches.

What are the San rock paintings at Baboon's Cave like?

Baboon's Cave (also known as Elands Bay Cave) contains an archaeological record spanning hundreds of thousands of years of human habitation. The visible rock art includes eland, human figures, and abstract San spiritual imagery. It is a UNESCO World Heritage-listed site reflecting both natural and cultural significance.

Are there penguins near Elands Bay?

There are no established penguin colonies at Elands Bay. The nearest African penguin colony is at Stony Point, Betty's Bay, approximately 200km south via the R27. The Verlorenvlei wetland adjacent to town is the main wildlife draw — a major birding destination.
