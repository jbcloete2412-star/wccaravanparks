About Oppi Dam

Dramatic setting at the foot of the majestic Swartberg Mountains in the picturesque Schoemanshoek Valley, en route to the world-famous Cango Caves. An intimate campsite for those wanting to explore Oudtshoorn's ostriches, caves and Karoo landscapes from a peaceful base.

Facilities & What to Expect

Oppi Dam Caravan Park sits beside a farm dam on the Klein Karoo plateau above Oudtshoorn — a relaxed, unfussy campsite that serves as the ideal base for exploring the Swartberg Pass and the surrounding Klein Karoo. Electric sites are available, ablution blocks are clean and functional, braai facilities are provided, and the dam-side setting adds a picturesque element to what is otherwise a practical campsite. The surrounding landscape of succulent karoo scrub, red earth, and towering Swartberg mountains is the real draw.

When to Visit

March through May and August through October offer the best temperatures — the Klein Karoo valley can reach 40°C in midsummer and experiences cold winters (frost is possible in June and July). Spring (September to October) is particularly beautiful when succulent plants flower after winter rains. Avoid camping in mid-December through January heat without excellent shade at your site.

What to Do Nearby

The Cango Caves (15km west of Oudtshoorn) are one of South Africa's most visited natural attractions — massive stalactite and stalagmite systems accessible via guided tours. The Swartberg Pass is one of the most dramatic mountain passes in Africa: 24km of gravel switchbacks through the Swartberg mountains with views that justify the drive in themselves — allow 2 hours and don't rush it. Ostrich farms around Oudtshoorn offer show farm experiences. Meiringspoort (30km north) is a stunning canyon drive through the Swartberg foothills.

Getting There

Oudtshoorn is on the R62 in the Klein Karoo, approximately 420km from Cape Town via the N1 to Touws River and then the R62. Alternatively take the N2 to George and then over Outeniqua Pass — 450km but a more gradual approach. The park is 10km east of Oudtshoorn on the R328.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Swartberg Pass like to drive?

The Swartberg Pass is an unfenced, unsealed (gravel) mountain pass rising to 1,583m above sea level. Hand-built in the 1880s and a national monument, it requires slow, careful driving — not suitable for large vehicles with trailers on the steepest sections. Allow at least 1.5 hours one-way. The views are extraordinary in every season.

What is the Cango Caves experience like?

Cango Caves is South Africa's most visited show cave — a vast system of calcite chambers with formations up to millions of years old. A standard heritage tour walks through the main chambers. An adventure tour option includes tight squeezes through narrow passages, suitable for the fit and not claustrophobic. The caves maintain a constant 18°C regardless of outside heat.

Is the Klein Karoo good for birdwatching?

The Klein Karoo has excellent and distinctive birdlife including Karoo korhaan, Namaqua warbler, rufous-eared warbler, and various larks and pipits. The Swartberg range adds mountain species. A Sasol bird app or field guide to Karoo birds will help significantly.
