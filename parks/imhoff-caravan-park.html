About Imhoff Caravan Park

A beloved 3-star resort on the Atlantic seaboard in the historic village of Kommetjie, just 150m from Long Beach. Grassed, electrified stands surrounded by trees and fynbos, with clear views of Chapman's Peak. Features chalets, games room, kids playground and secure access control.

Facilities & What to Expect

Imhoff Caravan Park in Kommetjie holds a 3-star rating and sits 150 metres from Long Beach — one of the longest and wildest beaches on the Cape Peninsula. The park is well-maintained with grassed electric sites, clean ablution facilities, and a calm atmosphere that reflects the village's unhurried character. Kommetjie is a genuine surf town and the park attracts a mix of surfers, families, and hikers using it as a base for the southern Cape Peninsula. It is significantly quieter than the Atlantic Seaboard parks closer to Cape Town.

When to Visit

Long Beach is swimmable from November through March when the south-easter is lighter in the early mornings and late afternoons. Winter (April through September) brings the consistent north-west swell that draws serious surfers. Chapman's Peak and the hiking trails are better in cooler months when the fynbos is in flower and the fire risk is low. Summer weekends can be busy — arrive Friday for the best sites.

What to Do Nearby

Long Beach stretches 6km and is one of the best kite surfing locations in South Africa — the right-hand side of the beach is dedicated to kite surfers. Chapman's Peak Drive is a 15-minute drive north along the coast. Cape Point and the Cape of Good Hope are 25km south — a full-day outing that should include the Cape of Good Hope hiking trail, the lighthouse, and the resident baboon troops. Imhoff Farm Village (1km from the park) has craft shops, a restaurant, and a small petting zoo. Boulders Beach penguins are 30km away via Simon's Town.

Getting There

From Cape Town take the M3 south toward Muizenberg, then continue via the M64 to Sun Valley and Kommetjie — approximately 45km from the city centre, about 50 minutes in moderate traffic. The park is well-signposted in Kommetjie village.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Long Beach safe for children to swim?

Long Beach is exposed and can have strong shore break and currents, especially when the south-easter is blowing. The far western end of the beach (away from kite surfers) is calmer. Always supervise children and check conditions before entering. No lifeguard service operates on this beach.

Is there surfing near Imhoff Caravan Park?

Yes — Kommetjie itself has several breaks including Outside Log Miles, a powerful reef break suited to experienced surfers. Long Beach has gentler shore break suitable for beginners in smaller swells. Boards and wetsuits are available for hire in the village.

How far is Imhoff Caravan Park from central Cape Town?

Kommetjie is approximately 45km from central Cape Town — about a 50-minute drive in normal traffic. It is on the southern Cape Peninsula, so journey times from the Winelands or Garden Route are longer than the map distance suggests.
