About Slanghoek Mountain Resort

Front-row seats to the Winelands' most dramatic mountain scenery in the Slanghoek Valley. A well-kept resort with optional private hot tubs at select sites — a special treat after wine tasting along the R45. Perfect harvest-season base for vineyards, scenic drives and walking trails.

Facilities & What to Expect

Slanghoek Mountain Resort is the most resort-like camping experience in the Boland mountains — managed facilities with hot tubs, a swimming pool, a restaurant, and on-site accommodation options alongside the caravan and camping sites. The Slanghoek Valley setting is dramatically beautiful: enclosed by towering Winelands mountain ranges with vineyards in the valley floor. Electric sites are well-maintained, ablution blocks are clean and regularly serviced, and the overall standard reflects a professionally run facility rather than a basic municipal campsite.

When to Visit

February through April is outstanding — the grape harvest is underway in the surrounding Rawsonville vineyards, the valley is warm but not yet at full summer heat, and the mountain streams are running. August through October brings spectacular spring wildflowers to the mountain slopes. The hot tubs are year-round assets — particularly welcome in winter when the Slanghoek valley cools considerably in the evenings.

What to Do Nearby

The Du Toitskloof Pass (15km north toward Paarl) is a spectacular mountain drive with views back over the Slanghoek Valley. Wellington wine route (25km) covers boutique estates in a quieter setting than Stellenbosch. Paarl town (30km north) is worth a day visit for the Paarl Mountain Nature Reserve hiking, the Afrikaans Language Monument, and wine estates. Bain's Kloof Pass (30km) is an historic mountain pass with hiking and swimming holes in the Witte River.

Getting There

From Cape Town take the N1 to Paarl, then the R101 south through Wellington to Rawsonville. The resort is in the Slanghoek Valley — approximately 10km beyond Rawsonville on the R101. Total distance from Cape Town: approximately 110km.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the hot tubs at Slanghoek like?

The hot tubs at Slanghoek Mountain Resort are private or semi-private heated soaking pools — a draw particularly in the cooler months when mountain evenings turn cold. Availability is typically on a booking basis through the resort — confirm current pricing and booking requirements when making your camp reservation.

Can I visit wine estates from Slanghoek?

The Breedekloof wine route (around Rawsonville and Worcester) covers the immediate area. Wellington wine route is 25km and Stellenbosch and Franschhoek are 45-60km but easily reachable for day visits. Having your own vehicle is essential for exploring the wine routes.

Is Slanghoek suitable for hiking?

The surrounding Slanghoek mountains have several trails ranging from easy valley walks to more demanding mountain routes. The resort can advise on current trail conditions and recommended routes. For more structured hiking, Bain's Kloof Pass (30km) has established trails with permit requirements available through CapeNature.
