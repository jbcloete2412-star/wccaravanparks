About Gansbaai Caravan Park

Municipal caravan park in the heart of Gansbaai, the world capital of great white shark cage diving. 116 sites with electricity, ablution facilities, hikes, walks and fishing. Perfectly positioned to access whale watching (June–December), the Dyer Island penguin and seal colony, and the famous "Shark Alley" boat trips — all departing from nearby Kleinbaai harbour.

Facilities & What to Expect

Gansbaai's municipal caravan park sits in the heart of the world's shark cage diving capital — and despite its famous surroundings, the camp itself is refreshingly straightforward. The 116 sites are well-maintained, electric, and ablutions are kept clean by the municipality. It is a functional, affordable base for experiencing one of the world's most extraordinary marine wildlife destinations. A short walk brings you to the harbour where dive operators depart each morning.

When to Visit

Great white shark activity peaks from June to November when the sharks are most active around Dyer Island and Geyser Rock. Walker Bay's southern right whales are also present from June through December, making winter and spring the single best time for marine wildlife. For calmer weather and beach swimming, December through February is pleasanter — but the park fills quickly over Christmas. Crayfish season from November is another major drawcard.

What to Do Nearby

Shark cage diving is the obvious headline — operators work from Gansbaai harbour, and the boat trip to Dyer Island is genuinely unforgettable whether or not a great white appears. The island's Cape fur seal colony and resident African penguins are spectacular in their own right. De Kelders, 5km north, has some of the best land-based whale watching in the world from cliff-top viewpoints above Walker Bay. Hermanus (50km) is worth a half-day trip for its cliff path and whale-watching season events.

Getting There

Take the N2 from Cape Town to Bot River (130km), then the R43 south through Hermanus and onward to Gansbaai — total distance approximately 180km, around 2.5 hours. Alternatively take the R44 scenic coastal route from Gordon's Bay through Betty's Bay and Kleinmond — allow an extra hour but the coastal scenery is outstanding.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does shark cage diving cost in Gansbaai?

Cage diving prices vary by operator and change seasonally — contact operators directly for current pricing. Most include breakfast and light refreshments on the boat. Booking well in advance is strongly recommended for peak months (June to November). Operators typically have minimum booking requirements for groups.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer for shark cage diving?

No. The cage floats on the surface and participants do not need to be strong swimmers or dive-certified. A basic snorkel, mask, and wetsuit are provided by the operator. Height and health restrictions may apply for some operators — check specific requirements when booking.

Is whale watching from Gansbaai as good as from Hermanus?

Walker Bay is the feeding and nursery ground for southern right whales, and the sightings from De Kelders cliffs (5km from Gansbaai) are exceptional — arguably the closest to shore anywhere on the whale watching circuit. Both Gansbaai and Hermanus offer superb viewing and complement each other well.
