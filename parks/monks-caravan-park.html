About Monks Caravan Park

A small, intimate park of 22 electrified sites on the Knysna Lagoon, within easy walking distance of Knysna town, the waterfront and restaurants. Clean ablutions and a laid-back atmosphere. Ideal for those wanting to be in the heart of Knysna without the crowds of larger resorts.

Facilities & What to Expect

Monks Caravan Park is Knysna's most intimate camping option — 22 sites in a compact, carefully managed setting within easy walking distance of the Knysna Waterfront and town centre. Sites have electricity and the ablutions are well-kept. Being small means Monks has a community feel that larger parks lack, with guests getting to know neighbours over braai fires and shared sunset views. It is not the place for family groups wanting space and privacy; it is exactly right for couples or small families wanting Knysna access without resort pricing.

When to Visit

Monks suits year-round camping given its proximity to Knysna town amenities — you are never far from a restaurant, coffee shop, or the waterfront if the weather turns. October through April offers the best Garden Route weather. Knysna's famous Oyster Festival (typically July — confirm current dates as events can move) draws large crowds, making it a lively time to visit but accommodation fills months ahead.

What to Do Nearby

Walk to the Knysna Waterfront (15 minutes on foot) for fresh oysters and craft beer. The Heads viewpoint and the looped 4km hiking trail around the promontory is a 20-minute drive. Featherbed Nature Reserve (boat ferry from the Waterfront) is a private eco-reserve offering guided coastal hikes and guided lunch experiences. The Knysna Forest hiking trails, including the half-day Elephant Walk, depart from the outskirts of town. Knysna Elephant Park is 12km east.

Getting There

Monks is in central Knysna — navigate to Knysna town and follow signs. It is approximately 500km from Cape Town via the N2, a 5.5-hour drive. George airport is 70km west with daily flights from Johannesburg and Cape Town — flying and hiring a car is a practical alternative for shorter trips.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Monks Caravan Park different from the larger Knysna parks?

Size and location. Monks has only 22 sites, creating a genuinely intimate atmosphere. Its biggest advantage is proximity to Knysna town — you can walk to the waterfront, restaurants, and shops rather than driving. Most Knysna area parks require a car for every amenity run. Monks suits those who want the Knysna experience, not just a campsite near it.

Is parking available at Monks for a large caravan?

Given the compact size of the park, very large caravans or 5th-wheel rigs may face challenges. Phone ahead to describe your rig dimensions so the park can allocate the most suitable site. Standard-sized touring caravans and motorhomes are generally accommodated.

What is the Knysna Oyster Festival?

The Knysna Oyster Festival is an annual food and sport event typically held in July, centred on the town's celebrated oyster industry. Events include oyster eating competitions, restaurant specials, a running and cycling race, and various water-based activities on the lagoon. Accommodation in Knysna books out months in advance for the event dates.
