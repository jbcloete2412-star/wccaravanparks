About Onrus Caravan Park

Beloved municipal caravan park in the charming village of Onrus, metres from the Onrus River mouth and beach. A firm favourite with repeat visitors who come year after year for the relaxed atmosphere and proximity to Hermanus — the world's best land-based whale watching destination. Quiet Overberg village character with easy access to Hermanus restaurants and the Walker Bay whale route.

Facilities & What to Expect

Onrus Caravan Park is a beloved municipal facility in the charming village of Onrusrivier, directly at the Onrus River mouth where fresh and salt water meet in an estuary perfect for children to paddle. Sites are grassed, many have good shade from established trees, electricity is available, and the ablution blocks are maintained to a consistent standard. The park is metres from the beach and within walking distance of the village's small selection of coffee shops and a bakery. Hermanus and all its amenities is just 5km east.

When to Visit

September through December is the golden period — southern right whales are actively nursing calves in Walker Bay, and the Onrus to Hermanus cliff walk becomes one of the world's greatest whale watching paths. October and November typically produce the highest whale counts. December through January brings beach families. Onrus is more sheltered than the Hermanus clifftop and is often calmer in the south-easter conditions that affect the main town.

What to Do Nearby

The Hermanus Cliff Walk runs 12km from one end of Hermanus to the other, with world-class whale watching directly below the path from June through December. The Fernkloof Nature Reserve above Hermanus has 60km of hiking trails through mountain fynbos. Shark cage diving in Gansbaai (40km west) is a full day activity. The Harold Porter National Botanical Garden in Betty's Bay (35km west) is excellent for fynbos and birdwatching. De Kelders sea caves (40km west) are accessible at low tide.

Getting There

From Cape Town take the N2 east to Bot River, then the R43 south through Hermanus to Onrusrivier — approximately 130km, just under 2 hours. From Cape Town via the scenic R44 (Clarence Drive) through Gordon's Bay, Betty's Bay, and Kleinmond: add 30 minutes but the coastal scenery is spectacular.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Walker Bay and why is it famous for whales?

Walker Bay is a large sheltered bay between Danger Point and De Kelders, used by southern right whales as a nursery ground from June through December. The whales give birth and raise calves in the bay's calm, shallow waters. The combination of sheltered geography and whale behaviour makes shore-based sightings from the Hermanus cliff walk uniquely close and frequent.

Is Onrus quieter than Hermanus for camping?

Yes — Onrus is a small village with a quieter pace and lower profile than Hermanus town. The park attracts returning visitors who prefer the village atmosphere and the river-mouth setting. For first-time whale watching or if you want access to a wider range of restaurants and shops, Hermanus is 5km away and easily reachable.

Are there sharks near Onrus beach?

Walker Bay has a healthy great white shark population centred primarily around Dyer Island near Gansbaai (40km west). Shark activity near Onrus beach is very low — the beach is a sheltered, shallow estuary environment. Always observe any shark alerts or flag warnings at South African beaches.
