About Montagu Caravan Park

Set in an apricot orchard with panoramic views of the Cogmans Kloof Mountains. Midway between Cape Town and Oudtshoorn on the famous Route 62 — one of South Africa's great scenic drives. Features heated indoor pools, hot mineral springs nearby, padstalle, arts & crafts and top wine cellars. 80 shaded grassed sites, chalets, wooden huts and backpackers.

Facilities & What to Expect

Montagu Caravan Park is beautifully positioned in a working apricot orchard — trees provide exceptional natural shade and the setting gives the camp an organic, unhurried atmosphere entirely at odds with the commercial resort style. Sites are grassed and electric, ablution facilities are well-maintained by the municipality, and the mountain setting means evenings are dramatically cool even in summer. The park is within easy walking distance of Montagu's historic Main Street with its Cape Dutch buildings, restaurants, and wine cellars.

When to Visit

August through October is the absolute prime: spring blossom fills the apricot orchards, wildflowers carpet the surrounding mountains, and the Montagu-Cogmanskloof landscape is at its most photogenic. February to April is harvest and jam-making season — the apricot scent in the orchard is remarkable. December through January can be very hot (the valley regularly exceeds 38°C) — camp here in summer only if you plan to spend midday hours at Montagu Hot Springs or in a wine cellar.

What to Do Nearby

Montagu Hot Springs (2km from the park) is a natural thermal resort with warm mineral pools — perfect for an evening soak. Montagu is a world-class rock climbing destination with hundreds of bolted sport routes above the town, drawing climbers from across South Africa. The Route 62 wine route connects Montagu to Robertson and Barrydale — excellent cellar visits along the valley. Robertson Wine Valley (45km east) is one of South Africa's most productive wine-growing regions with estates open for tasting.

Getting There

From Cape Town take the N2 to Somerset West, then the R44 or N1 to Worcester, and the R60 to Robertson and Montagu — approximately 190km, around 2 hours. The scenic alternative via Franschhoek Pass adds time but is spectacular. GPS is reliable to Montagu.

Frequently Asked Questions

How hot does Montagu get in summer?

Very hot. The Montagu valley can reach 40°C or above in December and January. If camping in summer, bring a quality shade structure for your awning, site selection under the orchard trees is critical, and plan outdoor activities for early morning and evening. The hot springs provide a comfortable retreat during peak heat.

What is the rock climbing like at Montagu?

Montagu is considered one of the top sport climbing destinations in South Africa with over 300 bolted routes across multiple crags. Difficulties range from beginner-accessible grades to advanced multi-pitch routes. The Cogmanskloof Pass area and Montagu Mountain are the main climbing areas. Local climbing gear shops can provide route topos and guided climbing options.

Is Montagu on Route 62?

Yes — Montagu is one of the main stops on Route 62, the mountain alternative to the N2 connecting Cape Town to Port Elizabeth via the Klein Karoo. Other Route 62 highlights include Robertson, Bonnievale, Barrydale, and Oudtshoorn. The route passes through some of South Africa's most scenically dramatic mountain and valley landscapes.
