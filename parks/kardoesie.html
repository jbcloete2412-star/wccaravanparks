About Kardoesie

Perched at the top of the scenic Piekenierskloof Pass on the N7 — a natural gateway between Cape Town and the Cederberg. Kardoesie has been welcoming travellers and caravanners for decades, offering a tranquil mountain setting surrounded by citrus farms and fynbos, with spectacular pass views.

Facilities & What to Expect

Kardoesie is a small, farm-style campsite set on the slopes of the Piekenierskloof Pass — a gateway to the Cederberg mountains and the citrus country of Citrusdal. Facilities are basic and authentic: decent ablutions, electric sites, mountain stream access, braai facilities, and the kind of rugged, unspoilt atmosphere that draws people who want the mountains rather than the resorts. This is a functional stopover as much as a destination — it sits on the N7 route between Cape Town and Springbok, making it a natural overnight halt for travellers heading north.

When to Visit

Spring (August to October) is outstanding here — the surrounding fynbos is in full flower, temperatures are moderate, and the pass road is at its most dramatic. Avoid December and January when the Boland valleys can reach extreme heat. Winter (June to August) nights are cold at this altitude but days can be crisp and clear — ideal for hiking. The Cederberg's famous rooibos harvest peaks March to April if you want to visit the tea estates around Citrusdal.

What to Do Nearby

Piekenierskloof Pass itself is a worthwhile slow drive for mountain views over the Oliphants River Valley. Citrusdal (30km north) is the gateway to the Cederberg Wilderness Area — book a permit for camping and hiking through CapeNature. The Baths Hot Springs at Citrusdal (32km) is a popular thermal pool resort worth a recovery stop. The Cederberg Conservancy's rock art sites are accessible from Citrusdal. Sanddrif and Dwarsrivier farms in the Cederberg offer camping and hiking with overnight trail options.

Getting There

Kardoesie is on or just off the N7, approximately 130km north of Cape Town. From Cape Town take the N1 to the N7 junction (approximately 70km), then the N7 north through Malmesbury toward Citrusdal. The pass is approximately 60km north of the N1/N7 interchange.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 4WD required to reach Kardoesie?

No — Kardoesie is accessible on the N7 national route, which is tarred. Standard passenger vehicles can reach the site without difficulty. If you plan to explore deeper into the Cederberg on gravel tracks beyond the main pass road, a high-clearance vehicle is advisable.

What is the best route from Cape Town to the Cederberg?

The most direct route is the N1 to Paarl, then the N7 north through Malmesbury and over Piekenierskloof Pass to Citrusdal. Both this and the Bain's Kloof Pass alternative via Wellington are roughly 240km to Citrusdal. Kardoesie is at the southern approach via the N7.

Can I see wildflowers near Kardoesie?

Yes — the Piekenierskloof Mountain Pass and surrounding area is excellent for fynbos wildflowers from August through October. The Cederberg's diversity of proteas, ericas, and restios means the flowering season can extend later into November at higher altitudes. Check WildflowerMap.co.za for current flowering reports.
