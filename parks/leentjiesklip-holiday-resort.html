About Leentjiesklip Holiday Resort

One of the West Coast's most popular resorts, overlooking the world-famous Langebaan Lagoon with arguably the most beautiful sunsets in South Africa. 147 grassed stands (24 with private ablutions), excellent security. Superb for windsurfing, kitesurfing, swimming and spring wildflowers.

Facilities & What to Expect

Leentjiesklip Holiday Resort in Langebaan occupies a prime stretch of the Langebaan Lagoon's western shore — one of the most sheltered and beautiful bodies of water in South Africa. The 147 sites have electricity, ablution blocks are well-maintained, the resort has its own restaurant, and the lagoon frontage provides the shallow, warm, flat water that has made Langebaan famous for kitesurfing and family swimming. The West Coast National Park begins almost immediately north of the resort entrance.

When to Visit

August and September is the wildflower season — West Coast National Park's annual flower display draws visitors from across the world and Leentjiesklip makes an excellent base. December through February brings the reliable south-easter that kitesurfers specifically travel for — the lagoon is perfectly flat and the wind is consistent. October to November and March to May are ideal shoulder months for families: warm lagoon water, less wind, and manageable crowds.

What to Do Nearby

Langebaan Lagoon is the centrepiece — a vast, shallow bay sheltered enough for beginner kitesurfers and small children in the shallows, yet large enough for sailing and kayaking expeditions. West Coast National Park (2km north) has the annual wildflower circuit (August to September), excellent birdwatching, and the remote Churchhaven settlement accessible only through the park. Kitesurfing schools operate in the lagoon. Paternoster (50km north) is a whitewashed fishing village with excellent seafood restaurants.

Getting There

Take the R27 (West Coast Road) north from Cape Town for 120km to Langebaan. The town is clearly signposted from the R27. The resort is on the lagoon's western shore within Langebaan — the drive takes approximately 1.5 hours from central Cape Town.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Langebaan Lagoon good for beginner kitesurfers?

Yes — the Langebaan Lagoon is widely regarded as one of the best beginner kitesurfing locations in South Africa because of its flat water, consistent wind, and absence of ocean currents or surf. Several professional kitesurfing schools operate from the lagoon. Intermediate and advanced kitesurfers also use the lagoon, particularly the tidal channels.

When is the best time for wildflowers near Leentjiesklip?

West Coast National Park's wildflower display typically peaks in August through September, though the exact timing varies by year and rainfall. Check WildflowerMap.co.za or the park's social media for current bloom reports before travelling specifically for flowers.

How warm is the Langebaan Lagoon for swimming?

The Langebaan Lagoon is significantly warmer than the open Atlantic Ocean nearby — reaching 24-26°C in summer because the shallow water heats rapidly in the sun. This makes it dramatically more comfortable for swimming than Cape Town's Atlantic-facing beaches, where water temperatures rarely exceed 16°C.
