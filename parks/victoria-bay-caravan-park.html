About Victoria Bay Caravan Park

Widely regarded as one of South Africa's best-positioned caravan parks — with stands perched directly above the ocean offering 180° bay views. Vic Bay is a legendary right-hand point break for surfers. 30 electrified stands across two terraces (Western has private ablutions), dolphins and whale sightings regular.

Facilities & What to Expect

Victoria Bay Caravan Park has only 30 sites and occupies one of the most improbable and beautiful settings of any campsite in South Africa — squeezed between towering coastal cliffs and the bay itself, with 180-degree ocean views and a world-famous right-hand surf break as your immediate neighbour. Sites are compact, the ablutions are functional, and electricity is available. This is not a resort — it is a coveted pocket of space in a place that feels genuinely precious. Book as early as possible for any visit.

When to Visit

June through September is when the right-hand point break fires with consistency — south-westerly swells wrap around the headland and create a rideable wave embedded in South African surfing history. December and January are the family peak — the bay's tidal pool and calmer conditions make it accessible for non-surfers. The bay's proximity to George (8km) means rain days are manageable with town as backup.

What to Do Nearby

The surf break at Victoria Bay is the central attraction — one of the most photographed right-hand point breaks in South Africa, with surfing photographs of the bay appearing in international publications. The tidal pool at the bay is safe for children and non-surfers. George (8km) has excellent restaurants, a small international airport, and shopping. The Seven Passes Road — a scenic alternative to the N2 connecting Wilderness to Knysna via seven historic bridges — starts within 15km. Wilderness Beach and Ebb and Flow National Park (12km west) extend the day-trip options.

Getting There

From George take the N2 east toward Wilderness for 8km, then follow the clearly signposted Victoria Bay turn-off. The road descends steeply to the bay — approximately 5km from the N2 on a tarred but narrow road. Large vehicles and caravans should use caution on the descent. From Cape Town the drive is 430km via the N2.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Victoria Bay surf good for beginners?

The main right-hand point break at Victoria Bay is generally not recommended for beginners — the wave can be powerful and fast, particularly in medium-to-large swells. The beach break section can be gentler in smaller swells. Beginners wanting to learn are better served by surf schools in George or Mossel Bay.

Can I park a large caravan at Victoria Bay?

The steep, narrow access road to Victoria Bay is challenging for large caravans and motorhomes. The campsite is also compact with 30 sites. Phone the park ahead to discuss your rig dimensions — some sites accommodate caravans but options for very large configurations are limited.

Why is Victoria Bay famous in South African surfing?

Victoria Bay has been surfed since the 1960s and appears in many of South Africa's most iconic surf photography archives. The combination of the right-hand point break's geometry, the dramatic cliff backdrop, and the remote-feeling setting (despite being minutes from George) makes it a magnet for photographers and surfing history.
